Executive & Administrative Governance

State Hierarchy

The civil executive authority, advisory assemblies, and judicial organs established under Instrument II and Instrument VI of the realm.

Civil Head of State Administration

The High Commissioner & Executive Authority

I

The High Commissioner

Appointed by the Sovereign under Instrument VI (Margravial Chancery & State Administration Act). The High Commissioner acts as Chief Executive Officer of civil administration, overseeing government agencies and statutory compliance.

  • Direction of Civil Ministries & Agencies
  • Implementation of Statutory Instruments
  • Primary liaison to the Sovereign Head of State
II

The Executive Council

Chaired by the High Commissioner, the Executive Council serves as the supreme administrative cabinet. It oversees daily governance, treasury operations, digital infrastructure, and regulatory enforcement.

  • Chancery & Administrative Policy
  • Treasury & Fiscal Regulation (Instrument IX)
  • E-Residency Operations & Public Records
Legislative & Representative Bodies

The Consultative Council & Commissions

III

The Consultative Council

Comprising representative delegates, subject-matter experts, and designated public advisors who review proposed statutory amendments and submit policy recommendations to the Executive Council.

  • Statutory Policy Review
  • Public & E-Resident Representation
  • Civic Advice to the Sovereign
IV

Consultative Members

Distinguished citizens and verified E-Residents appointed to specialized sub-committees to advise on commerce, maritime affairs, environmental protection, and digital governance.

  • Committee Advisory Roles
  • Digital Governance Input
  • International Policy Working Groups
V

Judicature & Legal Organs

Established under Instrument VII (Judicature and Courts of Justice Act). Independent judicial magistrates enforce the penal code, resolve corporate disputes, and maintain constitutional compliance.

  • High Court of the Realm
  • Administrative & Commercial Tribunals
  • Magisterial Legal Supervision
