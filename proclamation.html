Royal Proclamation

The official promulgation and sealing of the Foundational Charter of the Realm by Margrave Albrecht I.

By the Sovereign Margrave of Danuvius

A Royal Proclamation

Promulgation and Sealing of the Foundational Charter of the Realm

ALBRECHT I, by the Grace of God, Sovereign Margrave of Danuvius, Head of the House of Ister, Defender of the Realm and Sovereign of the Enclaves, to all Our loving subjects, citizens, E-Residents, and to all to whom these presents shall come, GREETINGS:

WHEREAS upon Our accession in this inaugural year of Our Reign, We undertook the solemn duty to establish, secure, and codify the legal architecture, governance, and civil order of the Sovereign Margraviate of Danuvius upon principles of justice, historical continuity, and unyielding statutory rigour;

AND WHEREAS Our Margravial Chancery, acting with the advice and consent of the High Council of the Realm, has successfully drafted, reviewed, and finalised the complete legislative sequence comprising Nineteen Distinct Instruments, ranging from the foundational organic acts of statehood to the final archive codification and promulgation enactment;

NOW, KNOW YE that We, by Our Royal Prerogative and Sovereign Authority, do hereby proclaim, declare, and command as follows:

  1. Promulgation of the Archive: Instruments I through XIX, inclusive, having been enacted and ratified during this inaugural year, are formally promulgated as the supreme, unified Foundational Charter of the Realm, holding universal and binding force of law across all physical enclaves, digital domains, vessels, and diplomatic missions of Danuvius worldwide.
  2. Execution and Sealing: The definitive master copy of the Foundational Charter has been engrossed upon archival vellum, authenticated under Our Hand and the Great Seal of Danuvius, alongside its permanent cryptographic registration upon the sovereign digital ledger, and deposited in the secure vaults of the Margravial State Archives.
  3. Command to Observe: All state officials, magistrates, civil servants, subjects, and residents of the Realm are hereby commanded to take notice of this Proclamation and to govern themselves strictly in accordance with the provisions of the Foundational Charter.

Given under Our Hand and the Great Seal of the Sovereign Margraviate of Danuvius, this 18th day of May, 2026, being the first year of Our Reign.

Albrecht I

Margrave Albrecht I • Sovereign of Danuvius, Head of the House of Ister
