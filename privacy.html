Privacy Policy

Sovereign Margraviate of Danuvius — Official Administration

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Introduction

The Sovereign Margraviate of Danuvius ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal data you share with us through our official portal (`margraviate-danuvius.org`). This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, used, and safeguarded during your interaction with our administrative services, specifically regarding E-Residency applications.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an application for Free Basic E-Residency via our official forms, we collect the necessary credentials required for state registry and verification, including:

  • Full Title and Name
  • Email Address
  • Country of Residence
  • Age
  • Confirmation of your Declaration of Allegiance

3. How Your Information is Used

The data collected via our application portal is utilized solely for:

  • Processing and evaluating your request for E-Residency status.
  • Issuing your digital certificate of registry and establishing official records within the Margraviate administration.
  • Communicating official state notices or responses directly to your provided email address (`registry@margraviate-danuvius.org`).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any external commercial entities or foreign governments.

4. Data Transmission and Security

Application submissions are securely transmitted via encrypted protocols to our designated official registries. While we employ standard web security measures (including SSL encryption and secure routing via FormSubmit), no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

5. Rights and Disclaimers

E-Residency is a digital administrative status. As outlined in our official state guidelines, E-Residency does not confer physical right of abode, diplomatic protections, or territorial rights, which remain exclusive to full Citizens. You retain the right to inquire about your stored registry data by contacting our administration directly.

6. Contact Information

For any questions, concerns, or data inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or your application, you may contact the official registry at: registry@margraviate-danuvius.org.
